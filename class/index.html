An ordinary peer-to-peer transfer of tokens you already hold, to any other account in this class — teacher or student. This moves your own balance; it's different from "Distribute," which creates new tokens.

Adds a new student to the class: generates their wallets on both networks and registers those addresses on-chain, so they're allowed to hold and receive ClassChain Token. Share the username/password you set here with the student directly.

Create a student account

Mints brand-new tokens directly into a student's wallet — this is how funds enter the class in the first place. It doesn't draw from your own balance, which is why your balance can stay at 0 and this still works.
